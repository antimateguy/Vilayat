## Deadline and place

In Munich you should register your address as soon as possible after moving in; the legal time limit is 14 days. Registration is done at one of the city's citizens' offices (Bürgerbüro): Forstenrieder Allee, Pasing, Orleansplatz, Leonrodstraße, Scheidplatz or Ruppertstraße. Appointments are booked through the city's online system.

## Documents

Bring a valid identity document for every person who is registering, and the landlord's confirmation that you have moved in (Bestätigung des Wohnungsgebers). The confirmation states the address, the names of everyone moving in and the date of moving in.

## Cost and result

Registration is free of charge. After you register, the federal tax office sends your tax identification number by post within about two to four weeks.

## Online registration

Online registration through wohnsitzanmeldung.gov.de is possible for a main residence only.
