## Proof of financial resources

Students from outside the EU must prove that they can pay for their stay. Since 1 January 2025 the amount is 992 EUR per month, which is 11,904 EUR per year. Accepted proof includes a blocked account (Sperrkonto), proof of the parents' income or a recognised scholarship.

## Blocked account

Money paid into a blocked account cannot be touched until you have entered Germany. After arrival you receive a bank card and can withdraw a limited amount each month. Banks charge between 70 and 150 EUR to open a blocked account.

## Average spending

According to the 2023 social survey, students in Germany spend 876 EUR per month on average. The largest item is rent including utilities at 410 EUR, followed by food at 198 EUR and health insurance and medical costs at 100 EUR. Transport costs 89 EUR on average.

## Semester contribution and tuition

Every student pays a semester contribution of between 70 and 430 EUR, depending on the university. Most state universities charge no tuition fees for Bachelor's and most Master's programmes. Baden-Württemberg charges students from outside the EU 1,500 EUR per semester.
