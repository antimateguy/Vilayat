## Deutschlandticket

The Deutschlandticket has cost 63 EUR per month since January 2026. It is valid on local and regional public transport in all of Germany: underground (U-Bahn), suburban trains (S-Bahn), buses, trams and regional trains in second class. It is not valid on long-distance trains (ICE, IC, EC).

## Discount ticket for students in Bavaria

Students, trainees and people doing voluntary service in Bavaria can buy the Bavarian discount ticket (Ermäßigungsticket). In 2026 it costs 43 EUR per month and is valid throughout Germany like the Deutschlandticket.

## Subscription and cancelling

Both tickets are monthly subscriptions that can be cancelled at any time. A cancellation must be made by the 10th of a month to take effect for the following month.
