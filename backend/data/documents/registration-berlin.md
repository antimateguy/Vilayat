## Deadline and place

In Berlin you must register your address within 14 days of moving in. Registration is done at a citizens' office (Bürgeramt). There are citizens' offices in all twelve districts, and you may use any of them. You need an appointment for a visit in person.

## Documents

Bring a completed registration form (Anmeldeformular), proof of identity such as your passport, and the landlord's confirmation of moving in (Wohnungsgeberbestätigung). The confirmation is signed by the landlord and states the landlord's name and address, the date you moved in, the address of the flat and the names of the people who moved in.

## Cost and result

Registration is free of charge. You receive a registration confirmation (Meldebestätigung) that shows your personal data and your address.

## Online registration

Online registration exists, but only for German and EU citizens aged 18 or over who have an identity card with the online function switched on and a BundID account. Other students register in person.
