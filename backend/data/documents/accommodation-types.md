## Halls of residence

A room in a hall of residence is the cheapest option, at a little over 250 EUR per month on average. Places are scarce: there are just under 240,000 publicly funded rooms for about 2.5 million students. Apply as early as possible, ideally as soon as you receive your letter of acceptance. Most halls are run by the local student services organisation (Studierendenwerk).

## Shared flats

Almost a third of students live in a shared flat (Wohngemeinschaft, WG). A room in a shared flat is usually much cheaper than a flat of your own. A 20 square metre room including ancillary costs costs on average 554 EUR in Munich and 398 EUR in Berlin.

## A flat of your own

Flats are often rented unfurnished, sometimes even without a kitchen. A 30 square metre flat near the university, including ancillary costs, costs on average 802 EUR in Munich and 650 EUR in Berlin.

## Deposit

Landlords ask for a deposit (Kaution) when you sign the contract. Two to three times the monthly rent without ancillary costs is normal.

## Where to look, and the first weeks

Student services organisations and International Offices keep lists of rooms. Listing portals such as wg-gesucht.de, immobilienscout24.de and immowelt.de show rooms and flats on the open market. For the first weeks, short-term lets, furnished rooms, hostels and guesthouses are common stopgaps.
