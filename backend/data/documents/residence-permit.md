## Who needs a residence permit

Students who are not citizens of the EU, Iceland, Liechtenstein, Norway or Switzerland need a residence permit to study in Germany. Citizens of those countries do not need one.

## Order of the steps

First register your address at the residents' registration office within two weeks of finding accommodation. You receive a registration certificate. With that certificate you then apply for the residence permit at the foreigners' office (Ausländerbehörde) of your city.

## Documents for the residence permit

The foreigners' office asks for your passport with the visa, the certificate of matriculation from your university, the registration certificate for your address, proof of sufficient funds and proof of health insurance. Some offices also ask for passport photos, the tenancy agreement or a health certificate.

## Fee and validity

The fee is up to 100 EUR. A residence permit for studying is issued for up to two years. It must be extended before it runs out; an extension is again for up to two years and depends on normal progress in your studies.
