## Amount

The broadcasting fee for a private household is 18.36 EUR per month. It is usually paid every three months, which is 55.08 EUR.

## One flat, one fee

The fee is charged per flat, not per person and not per device. In a shared flat only one person pays, and the flatmates can split the amount among themselves. Every adult who registers an address is contacted by the contribution service, because the registration office passes the new address on.

## Exemptions

Students who receive BAföG and people who receive certain social benefits can apply to be exempted. People with a severe disability pay a reduced fee of 6.12 EUR per month.
