## Who runs the halls and how to apply

The halls of residence in Berlin are run by the student services organisation studierendenWERK BERLIN. You apply online through its dormitory portal and choose three halls you would like to live in.

## Documents

You need an admission or matriculation certificate from a Berlin university, the receipt showing that the semester fees are paid, and an identity card or passport. The documents are uploaded as scans. Apply once you have the admission certificate and have paid the semester fees.

## Staying on the waiting list

As long as you have no tenancy agreement, you receive an email every 30 days asking whether you are still interested. You must answer within 5 days, otherwise the application expires. For family flats the waiting time is more than three semesters.

## Advice in person

The InfoCenter Wohnen at Hardenbergstraße 34, 10623 Berlin answers questions about housing.
