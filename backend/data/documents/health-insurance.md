## Insurance is required

Everyone in Germany must have health insurance. That includes international students and students in preparatory courses. You have to show proof of insurance when you enrol or when you apply for a visa, depending on your country.

## Statutory and private insurance

There are statutory (gesetzliche) and private insurers. Statutory insurance is the usual and cheaper choice for students. It covers visits to a doctor, medicines, emergency treatment, ambulance transport and hospital stays.

## Cost and the age of 30

Students under 30 pay a reduced student rate of about 120 to 130 EUR per month with a statutory insurer. From the age of 30 the student rate ends and the normal, much higher rate applies.

## Students from the EU

Students from EU and EEA countries who have statutory insurance at home can keep it. They need a European Health Insurance Card (EHIC) and a certificate that they do not have to take out German statutory insurance.

## Help

The International Office of your university explains the rules and can help find doctors who speak English.
