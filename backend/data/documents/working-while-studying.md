## Students from outside the EU

Students from outside the EU and the EEA may work 140 full days or 280 half days per year. Working more than that needs approval from the Federal Employment Agency (Agentur für Arbeit) and the foreigners' office (Ausländerbehörde). Self-employed or freelance work needs permission from the foreigners' office. Breaking these rules can lead to losing the right to stay.

## Students from the EU

Students from the EU, Iceland, Liechtenstein, Norway and Switzerland may work without a limit on days, like German students. Anyone who works more than 20 hours a week must pay insurance contributions.

## Academic assistants

Jobs as an academic or student assistant at a university are not counted towards the 140 days: there is no limit on the days. The foreigners' office must still be told about the job.

## Pay and insurance

The statutory minimum wage has been 13.90 EUR per hour since January 2026. Students who work more than 20 hours a week during term pay health, unemployment and nursing care insurance contributions. No contributions are due for work that lasts less than three months or less than 70 days in a year.
